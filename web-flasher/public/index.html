Troubleshooting & First-Time Setup

Putting a brand-new ESP32-C3 into Bootloader Mode

  1. Hold the BOOT button on the ESP32-C3 Super Mini.
  2. While holding BOOT, tap RST (or plug in the USB-C cable).
  3. Release BOOT, then click Install Firmware via USB.
  4. Once flashing completes, tap RST once to boot into Setup Mode.

First-Time Wi-Fi Setup

  1. Connect your phone or computer to the ESP32-FlightClock Wi-Fi network.
  2. Open http://192.168.4.1 if the captive portal does not open automatically.
  3. Enter your 2.4 GHz Wi-Fi credentials, GPS coordinates, and timezone, then click Save & Apply.
